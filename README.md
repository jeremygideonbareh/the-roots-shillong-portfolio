# The Roots Shillong

A photo-led portfolio of The Roots – Ki Thied Tynrai, a creative production house in Shillong. Open `index.html` or serve the repository root with any static server. GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main`.

## Portfolio

The site includes all 84 gallery items from the six disciplines on [The Roots’ public portfolio](https://www.therootsshillong.com/), including seven posters linked to their original films or broadcasts. Each record in `collection.js` identifies the originating page and asset. The logo and portfolio media are credited to The Roots and their respective creators. The studio’s [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/therootsshillong/) is linked separately.

## Design references

- The ivory (`#F8F9F4`) and black (`#000000`) palette and horizontal gallery reference come from [Natascha Vavrina on Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/natascha-vavrina).
- Project browsing and full-frame viewing draw on [Gavin Schneider Productions](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gavin-schneider-productions).
- The location photography sequence draws on [PSP Production](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/psp-production).

## Interaction

GSAP and ScrollTrigger drive the pinned project sequence and parallax. Previous and next controls, keyboard navigation and touch gestures provide alternative ways to explore. Reduced-motion preferences keep the gallery in native horizontal scrolling mode. The full-size image viewer supports the arrow keys and Escape.

DM Sans and Instrument Serif are served locally under the SIL Open Font License. GSAP and ScrollTrigger are bundled with their distribution notices.
